About me

Kathryn Walsh

Clinical psychology is about creating space to understand what is happening, how experiences connect, and what may help things feel different.

A calm, collaborative approach

Kathryn’s approach is thoughtful, compassionate and collaborative. The aim is to create a space where difficult experiences can be explored carefully, at a pace that feels manageable.
